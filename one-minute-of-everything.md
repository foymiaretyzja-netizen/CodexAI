# One Minute of Everything

## Core Direction
- **Format:** 2D side-view animation.
- **Camera:** Continuous horizontal slide from left to right, never stopping.
- **Transitions:** Seamless morphs only; no hard cuts.
- **Runtime:** 60 seconds total.
- **Subtitles:** Always visible at bottom center; minimal, poetic, never explanatory.
- **Timeline Bar (Top):** Always visible, showing relative time ("X years ago") and counting forward to **Now**.

## Global Visual Rules
1. The camera never stops moving horizontally.
2. Older eras drift off-screen left as new eras emerge right.
3. Environments morph organically rather than snapping.
4. Color palette gradually evolves with time:
   - Cosmic black/red-orange magma
   - Dark volcanic blues/gray
   - Marine deep blue/teal
   - Green-brown terrestrial tones
   - Industrial grays/neons
5. Subtitle style remains consistent: short, poetic, no exposition.

## 60-Second Timeline Script

### 0–5s — Formation of Earth and Moon
- **Timeline (top):** `4.6 billion years ago`
- **Visuals:**
  - Black space with drifting dust and glowing particles.
  - Molten proto-Earth accretes from debris.
  - A massive impact ejects fragments in spiraling arcs.
  - Fragments gather and stabilize into the Moon’s orbit.
- **Subtitle (bottom center):**
  - “From chaos, weight gathered.”

### 5–9s — First Rain and Atmosphere
- **Timeline:** `4.4 billion years ago`
- **Visuals:**
  - Earth cools from incandescent red to cracked dark crust.
  - Thick volcanic clouds swell overhead.
  - Torrential rain begins; steam rises in sheets.
  - Surface fissures fill with water.
- **Subtitle:**
  - “The sky learned how to fall.”

### 9–14s — Birth of Oceans
- **Timeline:** `4.2 billion years ago`
- **Visuals:**
  - Camera slides into vast dark oceans.
  - Waves gradually calm.
  - Faint sunlight pierces upper layers.
- **Subtitle:**
  - “Water claimed the surface.”

### 14–18s — First Microbial Life
- **Timeline:** `3.8 billion years ago`
- **Visuals:**
  - Transition to microscopic scale.
  - Simple cells drift, divide, and multiply.
  - Gas bubbles rise through liquid.
- **Subtitle:**
  - “Life began without permission.”

### 18–22s — Multicellular Life
- **Timeline:** `1.5 billion years ago`
- **Visuals:**
  - Cells cluster and differentiate.
  - Soft-bodied, alien marine organisms drift by.
  - Gentle bioluminescence pulses in the water.
- **Subtitle:**
  - “Many became one.”

### 22–25s — Sky Turns Blue
- **Timeline:** `2.4 billion years ago`
- **Visuals:**
  - Atmospheric color shifts from murky orange to clear blue.
  - Light intensifies across sea surface.
  - Oxygen haze spreads in thin luminous bands.
- **Subtitle:**
  - “The planet learned to breathe.”

### 25–29s — Fish Walk Onto Land
- **Timeline:** `375 million years ago`
- **Visuals:**
  - Shallow swamp shoreline appears.
  - Fish with limb-like fins pull onto mud.
  - Early plants sway along wet banks.
- **Subtitle:**
  - “The ocean let go.”

### 29–32s — Giant Fungi Era
- **Timeline:** `300 million years ago`
- **Visuals:**
  - Towering fungi rise over dense humid terrain.
  - Spores drift through thick air.
  - Forest forms feel alien and oversized.
- **Subtitle:**
  - “The land belonged to strangers.”

### 32–36s — Forests and Predation
- **Timeline:** `280 million years ago`
- **Visuals:**
  - True trees surge upward in layered depth.
  - Giant insects cross foreground and midground.
  - Carnivores pursue prey through undergrowth.
- **Subtitle:**
  - “Life learned to chase.”

### 36–41s — Dinosaurs
- **Timeline:** `200 million years ago`
- **Visuals:**
  - Early dinosaurs roam fern-heavy plains.
  - Volcanic silhouettes and ash-lit horizons.
  - Massive creatures dominate the frame as camera glides.
- **Subtitle:**
  - “Giants ruled without knowing it.”

### 41–44s — Extinction Events
- **Timeline:** `250–66 million years ago`
- **Visuals:**
  - Volcanic eruptions and darkened skies.
  - Forests wither and collapse.
  - Species silhouettes fade into dust.
- **Subtitle:**
  - “Most endings were quiet.”

### 44–47s — Dinosaur-Killing Meteor
- **Timeline:** `66 million years ago`
- **Visuals:**
  - Bright meteor streaks across sky.
  - White-hot impact flash fills the frame.
  - Shockwave clears the scene in a sweeping wipe.
- **Subtitle:**
  - “This one was not.”

### 47–51s — Ice Age
- **Timeline:** `20,000 years ago`
- **Visuals:**
  - Frozen plains and windblown snow.
  - Glaciers slide in the background.
  - Mammoths cross slowly in silhouette.
- **Subtitle:**
  - “Cold tested memory.”

### 51–55s — First Humans
- **Timeline:** `200,000 years ago`
- **Visuals:**
  - Small human groups gather around firelight.
  - Cave painting motifs briefly overlay rock walls.
  - Simple tools and hand-axes appear in motion.
- **Subtitle:**
  - “They watched the sky.”

### 55–60s — Modern Age
- **Timeline:** `Present day` → `Now`
- **Visuals:**
  - Settlements morph into towns, then dense cities.
  - Roads, towers, power lines, aircraft, satellites.
  - Transition to night skyline with glowing city grids.
  - Camera keeps sliding past skyscrapers.
- **Subtitle:**
  - “And decided to change everything.”

---

## Ending Beat
- Timeline reaches **Now** exactly at 60s.
- Camera does **not** stop; history continues beyond frame.
- Fade to black occurs mid-scroll, preserving momentum.
